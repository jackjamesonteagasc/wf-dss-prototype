These crops are recorded for later, per product-owner instruction (2026-10-20). The 2026 Teagasc Crops Costs and Returns booklet contains cost and margin tables for them, but adding them now would also require:

- confirmed target/placeholder yields (the PDF's non-cereal yield table is ambiguous in text extraction and needs a visual check);
- distinct machinery sets and operations (planters, harvesters, storage/drying), which the current operation catalogue does not model;
- a decision on the enterprise boundary (for example, maincrop potatoes are often grown with irrigation and contract storage).
